The credit sequence has four pages: S3PehR, Godot Engine for Browsers, AI credits, and code/deployment/hosting. All five creative AI tools share one page; GitHub, GitHub Copilot Fusion and Cloudflare Pages share another.

The November 20 artwork additions include ten scene-specific character portraits with matching facial animation and three happy-ending paintings reserved for the completion film. The film uses the existing **Epilogue (BGM)** recording exclusively; no new music was generated. Portrait source records are in `assets/characters/campaign/variations.json`, and original ending paintings are in `assets/cinematics/campaign/masters/reward-*.png`.

The November 22 revision adds story paintings, scene bridges and story layers made with GPT Image from the room paintings and the character sheets: stills of key moments (`assets/cinematics/campaign/stills/`), one painting for each move between places (`assets/cinematics/campaign/bridges/`), people, sleepers and open doors painted into rooms for parts of the story (`assets/environments/campaign/layers/`), a standing portrait of Frank Mercer, and repainted plates for the one-year studio and two chapter films. No new music or voices were generated; scene transitions crossfade the existing ambience and music. The ninth-night hum (the choir under the bay, on Frank's radio and on his first and last reels) was generated with ElevenLabs Sound Effects; its masters, prompts and build notes are in `assets/audio/bay-hum/`.

A later November 22 pass repaints parts of two rooms with GPT Image (Chapter 1: empty coat hooks, a bolted balcony door and Mr. Bell's closed gate; Chapter 2: the gallery wall by the staff door), adds the hung raincoat as a layer and three Chapter 2 stills (pump room, service doors, holding room), and records five reworded Chapter 1 lines for Steve with ElevenLabs v4.

The five new evidence paintings also retain their original PNGs in `assets/cinematics/campaign/masters/`, using the same descriptive basenames as the optimized WebP inserts in `game/assets/illustrated/campaign/inserts/`.

The family recorder in the sleeping gallery and the live KBWR cue desk were painted with GPT Image using the corresponding room paintings as references. Their editable PNGs are `assets/cinematics/campaign/stills/c9_wake-family-recorder.png` and `c10_live-cue-desk.png`. Rebuild the runtime WebPs with `python3 tools/build-story-stills.py c9_wake-family-recorder c10_live-cue-desk`. The revised north-wing work-order painting retains the signature and 20:30 wall-clock time and now shows the staff ID someone could have misused. The Chapter 1 family dinner at La Marea (`assets/cinematics/campaign/masters/c1_dinner.png` -> `game/assets/illustrated/campaign/rooms/c1_dinner.webp`) was repainted with GPT Image from the room painting and approved cast references so the Reyes family, Steve, and Nora sit and eat together around the table with Rafa in the kitchen, Carmen bringing a warm dish, and Frank's chair and plate kept empty. Chapter 5's radio shack (`assets/cinematics/campaign/masters/c5_shack.png` -> `game/assets/illustrated/campaign/rooms/c5_shack.webp`) was similarly repainted with Luis Reyes naturally behind Frank's workbench, and Chapter 4's transfer-van sequence (`c4_escape-drill`, `c4_escape-motion`, and `c4_escape-rear-door`) was repainted with GPT Image to show Steve boarding uncuffed as a protected witness, riding in back behind the cab mesh, and slipping out unseen on the blind gravel bend.

The additional standing townspeople use the original full-length cast studies in `assets/characters/campaign/references/` and approved portraits in `masters/`. `tools/pack-talk-residents.py` prepares their transparent floor-registered WebPs (Pillow, NumPy and SciPy). `game/content/scene-people.json` places them in the paintings, traces the already-painted delegates and pod occupants, and identifies remote contacts. Figure marks are `[appearance, foot_x, foot_y, height, optional_flip]` in normalized scene coordinates. A speaker’s story ID stays distinct from the appearance they share with a copy.
