## How to read this

- Scenes appear in play order. Each scene has a short account of what happens, then one row per lead (the game's word for a puzzle or story step).
- **Solution** is the correct answer. **Why it works** points at the clue the player already has, and where that clue was planted.
- **Deduction** marks a graded puzzle with one right answer. A wrong pick shows a reason and costs a click, nothing more. **Beat** marks a moment the character commits to. It has one button and is never graded.
- Dates follow the calendar the script uses. Chapter 1's Saturday is the 20th of October. The ninth nights are October 21, October 30 and November 8.

## Who's who, at the start

- **Steve Mercer**, 35, the night sound engineer KBWR replaced with a machine three weeks ago. His parents vanished on the bay eighteen months ago, the night he said no to their boat trip.
- **Nora Reyes**, his girlfriend, head aquarist at the Bellwether Aquarium. She used to argue with everybody. Lately she has been endlessly patient.
- **Frank and Helen Mercer**, Steve's parents. Frank is a radio ham, call sign K7BLW. Helen is a retired government interpreter.
- **The Reyes family** runs La Marea: Carmen (Nora's mother), Luis (her father), and her brothers Mateo (firefighter, Little League coach), Rafa (chef) and Tomás (conspiracy podcaster).
- **Sheriff June Calder**, **Deputy Lyle Boone**, **Dale Kittredge** the mail carrier, **Gus Albano** the baker, **Mrs. Agnes Penhallow** (91, Steve's third-grade teacher, sells bait nobody buys), **Admiral** (the harbor sea lion Nora raised).
- **DJ AutoRick**, the cheerful machine that took Steve's job and reads yesterday's weather.

## True choices and deduction checks

- **There are no branching choices in the script.** Every scene and lead plays in the same order for every player, and the ending is fixed. Leads unlock by `requires` only.
- **88 leads are deduction checks.** Each has one right answer. A wrong pick shows a specific reason in the playing character's voice and costs a click. It changes nothing else.
- **13 leads are beats.** They are the moments where a character commits: Nora's irises, recording Veyr's departure, signing on with K7BLW, refusing Saren, letting Iris break the link, answering for the thirteen months, waking Rook with June's voice, opening Frank's note, putting Wen and Shaw on one cue sheet, and the four epilogue moments. They have one button and are never graded.
- **The notebook theories** (for example "Dad's old recorder is printing one tape onto the other") are the player's own hypotheses. Crossing one out has no story consequence.
