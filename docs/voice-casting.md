## How the voices were chosen

- Seven searches of the ElevenLabs library, one per group of characters, shortlisted four voices for each role from the character sheet in `docs/source-text/characters.txt` and the art prompts. A second round added five more per role, matched to each character's background and build. That made 8 to 10 options per role, each with its own preview clip.
- The casting page showed each character's portrait, line count by chapter and current in-game voice next to the candidates. You picked 25 roles there.
- Nora: you asked for the voice President Ward had before, a Higgsfield preset called "Sloane" that is not in the ElevenLabs library. Fifteen library voices were measured against Ward's five recorded lines on pitch, pitch range, brightness and timbre. "Sloane - Bold and Polished" was the closest by a clear margin and is cast for Nora and Iris.
- President Ward is now Deborah.
